story:
  - "I started making bags the way most people do — by taking one apart to see how it was built, then getting the pattern wrong three times before it finally sat right. That was a few years and a great many offcuts ago."
  - "Everything here is cut, stitched, and finished by hand at a workbench that looks out over the water. I keep runs small on purpose: it means every bag gets checked, adjusted, and finished properly before it goes anywhere."
  - "If you want something in a different size, colour, or leather, get in touch — most of what's shown here started as exactly that kind of conversation."
steps:
  - "Pattern is cut and checked against the hide for the best grain and fewest marks."
  - "Pieces are cut, edges beveled, and skived where they'll fold or overlap."
  - "Everything is hand-stitched with waxed thread, saddle-stitch style — two needles, one hole at a time."
  - "Hardware is set: buckles, rivets, and clasps fitted and tested under load."
  - "Edges are burnished and the whole bag is conditioned before it leaves the bench."
